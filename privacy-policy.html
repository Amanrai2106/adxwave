Information We Collect

We may collect personal and business information that you voluntarily share with us, including your name, phone number, email address, company name, service requirements, campaign goals, project details, and any files or messages you send during inquiries or onboarding.

  • Contact details submitted through forms, calls, WhatsApp, or email
  • Business details needed to discuss services, proposals, and project scope
  • Billing or transaction details when commercial services are purchased
  • Basic technical data such as browser, device, IP address, and referral source when available through hosting or analytics tools
02

How We Use Information

We use collected information only for legitimate business purposes connected to our digital marketing, branding, advertising, consulting, and website services.

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • To prepare proposals, service plans, estimates, and onboarding documents
  • To deliver SEO, advertising, creative, web, and growth services
  • To communicate updates, reports, invoices, and project milestones
  • To improve our website, service quality, and client support process
  • To meet legal, accounting, and contractual obligations
03

Sharing And Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information only when it is necessary to operate our business or deliver services requested by you.

  • With internal team members and approved contractors working on your project
  • With service providers such as hosting, analytics, payment, email, or ad platforms
  • When required by law, regulation, court order, or lawful government request
  • To protect our rights, enforce agreements, or prevent fraud and abuse
04

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for inquiry handling, client servicing, record keeping, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. If you stop engaging with us, we may keep limited business records, invoices, and communication history for administrative purposes.
